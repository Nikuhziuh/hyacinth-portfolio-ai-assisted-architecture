FINAL MASTER BLUEPRINT WITH COMMENTARY
Portfolio Website for Hyacinth Kaye Bajuyo

Core Intent
- Design a premium personal portfolio for Hyacinth Kaye Bajuyo with a soft, elegant, modern, feminine pearl-glass aesthetic.
- The site should feel editorial, airy, polished, and premium, with cinematic motion and strong consistency from Hero to Footer.
- The final result should feel cohesive across all sections, especially where real attachments, popups, PDFs, HTML samples, and image-based credential proof are involved.

Master Commentary
- Pages `1` to `6` are primarily design-system and layout-driven pages. They are important, but they are less risky because they are not heavily dependent on attachment logic.
- Pages `6.5`, `7`, and `8` are the most implementation-sensitive parts of the site because they rely on actual files, popup behavior, slide counts, document frames, and viewer-type rules.
- This blueprint is intentionally detailed so the final implementation does not drift into alternate naming systems, wrong popup models, or mismatched preview logic.

I. Global Design DNA

Palette Lock
- Strict palette lock:
  - blush pinks `#F6B1B1`, `#F8C6C6`
  - dusty rose / burgundy `#D97C7C`, `#7A1E1E`
  - peach / nude `#F3C7B5`, `#F5D5C8`
  - text `#2B2B2B`, `#6E6E6E`
- Use pearl white or warm off-white as the base background.
- Do not introduce purple, blue, orange, or unrelated pinks.
- Use `#F8C6C6` and `#F6B1B1` for translucent glass fills and highlights.
- Use `#F3C7B5` and `#F5D5C8` for blobs, halos, and gradient blooms.
- Use `#D97C7C` for borders, buttons, glow lines, interaction, and rose-tinted shadows.
- Use `#7A1E1E` only for selective premium depth and emphasis.

Typography DNA
- Use elegant serif for headlines.
- Use clean sans-serif for navigation, labels, pills, tabs, buttons, and body text.
- Headings should feel editorial and premium, not corporate or generic.
- Body text should feel refined but highly readable.

Glass Treatment
- Every glass element must use the site’s dual-edge treatment:
  - `1px solid rgba(255,255,255,0.2)`
  - `inset 0 0 10px rgba(255,255,255,0.1)`
- Where needed, add a soft outer `#D97C7C` glow-line.
- Keep glass elegant and luminous, but never so transparent that readability is damaged.

Background Language
- Backgrounds should be airy and minimal.
- Use low-opacity organic blobs and diffused gradient blooms.
- Keep them slightly blurred and atmospheric.
- The background should support the content, not overpower it.

Commentary
- This global design DNA is the visual source of truth for the whole site.
- Any future design refinement should stay inside this palette and material system unless the blueprint itself is intentionally revised.

IV. Hero to About Transition

Main Transition Concept
- Treat the Hero-to-About transition as one continuous cinematic scroll scene.
- Use a shared `180vh to 200vh` section with a `100vh` sticky viewport.
- Keep the Hero portrait and aura composition in a single motion group so they travel together into the About layout.

Scroll Phase 1
- `0% to 25%` scroll:
  - Hero text and CTA buttons fade, lift slightly, and soften out
  - opacity: `1 -> 0`
  - scale: `1 -> 0.96`
  - y: `0 -> -20px`
  - optional blur: `0 -> 4px`

Scroll Phase 2
- `25% to 65%` scroll:
  - portrait and aura enlarge slightly and shift into the future left-side About position
  - scale: `1 -> 1.18 to 1.25`
  - x: `0 -> -18vw to -22vw`
  - y: `0 -> 1vh to 2vh`

Scroll Phase 3
- `40% to 75%` scroll:
  - About card forms behind the arriving portrait with a soft glass build-in
  - opacity: `0 -> 1`
  - scale: `0.97 -> 1`
  - y: `36px -> 0`
  - border radius remains soft and generous around `32px to 40px`

Scroll Phase 4
- `55% to 100%` scroll:
  - About content glides in from the right in a staggered sequence
  - headline first
  - description second
  - badge or supporting detail last
  - x: `80px -> 0`
  - opacity: `0 -> 1`

Motion Style
- Motion should feel soft, elegant, and premium rather than dramatic.
- Use a smooth easing curve such as:
  - `cubic-bezier(0.22, 0.84, 0.2, 1)`
  - or a soft spring
- Include a reduced-motion fallback where the Hero fades out and the About section appears with minimal transform.

Commentary
- This transition is one of the most signature moments of the site.
- It should feel intentional and cinematic, but never at the expense of clarity or accessibility.

V. Responsive Rules

Responsive Layout
- On mobile, collapse `3`-column sections into a clean single-column stack.
- Preserve the glass aesthetic, spacing, and hover/tap polish.
- Keep the Hero-to-About transition active on mobile, but optimize it for touch and reduced viewport height.
- Maintain the same visual hierarchy, soft motion, and premium spacing at every breakpoint.
- Do not let the mobile site feel like a stripped-down afterthought.

Commentary
- The mobile version must still feel art-directed.
- Do not sacrifice polish just because the layout becomes simpler.

VI. Accessibility & Motion Safeguards

Touch Target Safety
- Maintain minimum touch targets of `44px x 44px` for:
  - buttons
  - links
  - arrows
  - close buttons
  - tabs
  - pills
  - interactive popup controls

Keyboard and Focus Safety
- Provide visible keyboard focus states for all interactive elements.
- Use a clear on-brand focus ring or glow that remains easy to see against glass backgrounds.

Contrast and Readability
- Ensure strong text contrast across all sections.
- Body text must remain clearly readable against glass, gradients, and blurred backgrounds.

Motion Safeguards
- Include reduced-motion fallbacks for:
  - scroll-driven cinematic effects
  - hover motion
  - floating cards
  - carousel movement
  - popup transitions
- When `prefers-reduced-motion` is enabled:
  - replace complex transforms and parallax with minimal fade / opacity transitions or static layouts
- Motion must never block usability, readability, or navigation.

Commentary
- Accessibility should not be treated as an afterthought.
- This site can still feel premium and cinematic while remaining comfortable to use.
